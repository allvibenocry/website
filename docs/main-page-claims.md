# The main page's claims

Every statement [site/index.html](../site/index.html) makes about the product,
with its source in the product repository and its status. The page is the
product's pitch, and says it is in development; this file says, claim by claim,
how much of the pitch the product repository supports (D21).

**Read at:** [allvibenocry/allvibenocry](https://github.com/allvibenocry/allvibenocry)
commit [`6451c59`](https://github.com/allvibenocry/allvibenocry/tree/6451c59f4eba276676e7640902f0b23c0d9e72e5), 2026-09-27, which holds the roadmap entries the
architect's review of the website added (D33 there). Source links point into
that commit.

**Statuses**, as on the Under the hood page:

- **Built**: built, and tested on the local test host; not yet on real
  hardware, and not yet tried by the owner.
- **Planned**: an entry of the product's roadmap, or later work named in its
  CLAUDE.md or a decision. Nothing of it is built.

A claim that is partly built and partly planned is split, or carries the status
of its weakest part. Claims the product repository does not support at all, not
even as planned, have no source and are listed at the end, with the claims that
are supported only in part. None of them is given a source it does not have.
